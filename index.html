A mind in motion

Between biological intelligence and the computational ideas that help us understand it.

I work across computational neuroscience, machine learning and neuroimaging. I am interested in models that do more than fit data: models that help us ask better questions about how brains work.

Outside the lab, I follow questions across philosophy, consciousness, science and the history of ideas. Curiosity is not a side project. It is part of the method.
