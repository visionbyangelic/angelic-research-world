Connectome-to-Behavior

Ongoing

Can natural coordinated behaviors emerge organically when real synaptic wiring is given simple biophysical rules and placed in an embodied physical simulation?

TechnicalClosed-loop simulation coupling complete Drosophila connectomes (FlyWire, BANC) with Brian2 point-neuron dynamics and an embodied biomechanical body (MuJoCo, flygym) to evaluate emergent locomotion, grooming, and feeding without hand-coded motor rules.

Plain languageTake the full wiring diagram of a fruit fly’s brain, switch it on inside a computer, give it a virtual body, and see whether it walks, cleans itself and feeds, without anyone programming those moves. No findings yet.

EM-NAV

Completed

How do biological constraints shape the maps an artificial navigator builds?

TechnicalComputational study examining how biological constraints (spiking thresholds, metabolic sparsity penalties, and recurrence) shape internal spatial representations, place-cell-like geometry, and zero-shot transfer stability in embodied agents under severe sensory ambiguity. Key Finding: Constrained spiking networks produced place-cell-like coding with up to ~80x higher spatial information per spike than unconstrained baselines and demonstrated superior transferability into continuous 3D environments.

Plain languageGive a virtual explorer a brain that must save energy and only fire when it counts, like a real one. Its inner “map” starts to look like the place cells in real brains, and it copes better when dropped somewhere new.

Brain Aging Dynamics

Accepted · Oral, NSN 2026

TechnicalStructural MRI morphometrics across OpenBHB and OASIS-3 cohorts. Accepted for Oral Presentation at NSN 2026.

Plain languageTeach a computer what a healthy brain looks like at each age from MRI scans. Then show it someone new: if their brain looks older than their birthday says, that gap may be a clue to health.

Model-based control & the brain

Proposed

TechnicalAnalysis plan relating structural and task-fMRI predictors to model-based control, using the OpenNeuro ds007474 two-step dataset.

Plain languageSome people plan ahead; others run on habit. Do their brains look or work differently? A plan only, no results.

TechnicalLeaky integrate-and-fire neuron: membrane potential integrates input current through resistance Rm and leaks toward rest; crossing −50 mV emits a spike and resets.

Plain languageA neuron is a bucket with a small hole. Input fills it, the hole leaks it, and when it overflows the cell shouts once and empties.
